# Critique loop (scores 1-10: hook / readability on a phone / motion / variety / brand + truth / sound)

What the rejected first X cut taught: small full-window UI shots that nobody can read on a phone, a long typing stretch,
a keyboard sound on every keystroke plus bright whooshes, and a wordy feature tour. This cut answers each one: a story
instead of a tour, one big sentence instead of watching it being typed, 20 soft sounds in 44 s (72 % of the film is silent),
and every real capture shown at 1.0x with a large caption that carries the meaning.

## Round 1: first full contact sheets (16:9) + stills of every scene
Scores 6 / 6 / 7 / 7 / 8 / -
Worst three: (1) the run was a small character in a 1180x580 crop of the 1.25 UI take (about 450 px tall); (2) the turn and
pain captions sat on the mannequin's head, and the frame counter hid behind the curve view; (3) the "free camera" and
"AI on your device" beats were small cards floating in an empty frame, the end card's «تعال لـ Blazma» left «لـ» standing alone.
Fixed: a new take at UI scale 1.0 with a tighter camera box (character about 590 px tall, crop 1200x700 at 1.0x); the mannequin moved
left and down, the counter and curve view re-laid; the end line is «تعال جرّب Blazma»; the feature beats were re-composed.

## Round 2: contact + phone sheets in both formats
Scores 8 / 7 / 8 / 8 / 8 / 8
Worst three: (1) the app's own monospace line «Kimodo (ذكاء اصطناعي محلي)» was the centre of the AI beat, and its Arabic
spacing looks odd in a close-up; (2) the tutorial beat was a dim, small card that said little; (3) every feature beat
started from a black frame (fade in), and in 1:1 the plan crop cut the green «مفهومة» (understood) labels off.
Fixed: the AI beat uses the dialog's own «Kimodo: متاح / يعمل على هذا الحاسوب» lines + the real device list; the tutorial beat
became "understands Arabic, even dialect", showing the real plan of the dialect sentence with all three steps «مفهومة» and the three
step words as a sub-line; captures are on screen from their first frame; 1:1 crops moved so the labels and the whole prompt box are in.

## Round 3: frames pulled from the encoded mp4s at every caption, both formats; loudness and spectrum of the mix
Scores 8 / 8 / 8 / 8 / 9 / 8
Every caption checked in the encoded frames: joined letters, right-to-left order, «؟» and «!» on the left end, «..» trailing, shadda in
«تحرّك», «جرّب», «حمّله», «يولّد». The note «(وقت الانتظار مختصر)» moved off the dialog's top edge. The pop and the thud are
placed from the measured take seconds (start 24.42 s, landing 31.15 s). Chime and pop lowered so the fall stays the
strongest sound. Master: -18.0 LUFS integrated, true peak -2.8 dBTP on the AAC files; spectral centroid of every sound
under 800 Hz, almost nothing above 4 kHz.
Left to improve: the app's UI text inside the real captures is still small on a phone (only the captions are readable there;
nothing is upscaled); the AI beat is the visually weakest; the dialog's top edge shows a dimmed hover tooltip of the real app.
