Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable (vc_redist.x64.exe)

The Windows installer (not the portable zip, not the installed app folder) carries the unmodified, Microsoft-signed
"Microsoft Visual C++ 2015-2022 Redistributable (x64)" downloaded from https://aka.ms/vs/17/release/vc_redist.x64.exe.
It is installed only when the runtime is missing, because tools\kimodo\kimodo-runner.exe and its GGML libraries are
built with MSVC and need MSVCP140.dll, VCRUNTIME140.dll and VCRUNTIME140_1.dll.
